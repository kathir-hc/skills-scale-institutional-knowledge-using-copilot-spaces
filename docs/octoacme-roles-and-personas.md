# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Clarify requirements, discuss technical trade-offs, estimate effort for features
- **Project Managers**: Report progress, flag risks and blockers, coordinate dependencies
- **QA/Testing**: Collaborate on test cases, reproduce and fix bugs, improve test automation
- **UX Designer**: Review design feasibility, implement visual specifications, provide feedback on user flows
- **Business Analyst**: Ask requirement questions, validate business logic implementation

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on technical trade-offs, prioritize features, validate delivered solutions
- **Project Managers**: Align on roadmap timing, coordinate stakeholder communications, balance scope and timeline
- **Business Analyst**: Refine product vision into detailed requirements, validate business value
- **UX Designer**: Define user needs and business goals, review designs for product alignment
- **Stakeholders**: Present roadmap, gather feedback, validate outcomes and measure success

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate timelines, manage dependencies, track progress and blockers
- **Product Managers**: Align on roadmap timing, coordinate stakeholder communications, manage scope
- **Scrum Master**: Align on delivery timelines, coordinate cross-team dependencies, share team metrics
- **Release Manager**: Align release timing with project milestones and dependencies
- **Stakeholders**: Provide status updates, manage expectations, escalate risks and decisions

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals validate that features meet acceptance criteria, quality standards, and user expectations. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure reliable, high-quality deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans and test cases
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Report and track defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in test automation and continuous integration
- Collaborate on definition of done and quality gates

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and reliability
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Build confidence in releases through comprehensive testing
- Improve test coverage and automation over time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup participation
- Bug reports and status updates in issue trackers
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Release readiness assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test cases, reproduce and verify bugs, pair on test automation
- **Product Managers**: Validate features meet acceptance criteria, provide quality feedback on user stories
- **Project Managers**: Report testing status, flag blockers and risks affecting timelines
- **Release Manager**: Coordinate test cycles, sign off on release readiness
- **Business Analyst**: Review requirements for testability, validate business logic

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile team ceremonies and practices, remove impediments, and coach teams on continuous improvement. They serve as servant leaders who enable teams to deliver value efficiently and sustainably.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate scrum ceremonies (daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, reviews)
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Shield the team from external distractions and interruptions
- Track and improve team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of transparency, inspection, and adaptation

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and delivery rhythm
- Build self-organizing, high-performing teams
- Continuously improve processes and practices
- Maintain healthy team dynamics and collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Daily facilitation of standup meetings
- Sprint planning and retrospective sessions
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Impediment tracking and escalation as needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coach on estimation, remove blockers, facilitate technical discussions
- **Product Managers**: Help refine backlog, ensure clarity of requirements, protect team capacity
- **Project Managers**: Align on delivery timelines, coordinate dependencies, share team metrics
- **QA/Testing**: Facilitate test planning, ensure quality practices are integrated
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate team progress, manage expectations on delivery cadence

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers coordinate organizational changes affecting processes, tools, or team structures. They assess impacts, communicate changes effectively, and ensure smooth transitions through documentation and stakeholder engagement.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct impact assessments for proposed changes
- Develop and execute change management plans
- Communicate changes to all affected stakeholders
- Manage documentation and approval workflows for changes
- Monitor adoption and address resistance or concerns
- Coordinate training and support during transitions

### Goals
- Minimize disruption during organizational changes
- Ensure high adoption rates for new processes or tools
- Maintain clear change history and decision rationale
- Build change-ready culture and resilience

### Typical Communication
- Change notifications and impact assessments
- Training sessions and knowledge transfer
- Status updates on change adoption
- Stakeholder feedback collection and analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate change timing with project milestones, assess project-level impacts
- **Product Managers**: Communicate product changes to users and stakeholders
- **Developers**: Coordinate tooling and process changes, gather technical impact feedback
- **Scrum Master**: Align on team process changes, support adoption of new practices
- **Stakeholders**: Manage expectations, gather requirements, communicate benefits and timelines

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions by eliciting requirements, documenting specifications, and validating that implementations meet business objectives. They translate stakeholder needs into actionable, testable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Create detailed functional specifications and user stories
- Define and validate acceptance criteria with stakeholders
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Support UAT (User Acceptance Testing) coordination
- Maintain traceability between requirements and deliverables

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions align with business needs
- Reduce ambiguity and rework through clear requirements
- Facilitate shared understanding across business and technical teams
- Validate that delivered features achieve intended business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User story documentation and acceptance criteria
- Business process diagrams and workflow documentation
- UAT coordination and validation sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Partner on roadmap items, refine product vision into detailed requirements
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements, answer questions during implementation, review demos
- **QA/Testing**: Define test scenarios based on business logic, support UAT coordination
- **Stakeholders**: Elicit needs, validate solutions, manage requirement changes
- **UX Designer**: Collaborate on user flows and experience requirements

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-centered experiences through research, wireframes, prototypes, and design systems. They ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and delightful while balancing user needs with business and technical constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Design user flows and information architecture
- Maintain design systems and component libraries
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design practices
- Collaborate on design reviews and iterations

### Goals
- Create intuitive, user-friendly experiences
- Reduce user friction and support task completion
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Advocate for user needs throughout development

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Design specifications and handoff documentation
- Collaborative design sessions with product and engineering

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Validate product vision, align on user needs and business goals
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation feasibility, provide design specs and assets
- **Business Analyst**: Define user workflows, validate business process alignment
- **QA/Testing**: Review usability, validate accessibility compliance
- **Stakeholders**: Present design concepts, gather feedback, communicate design decisions

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and execute software releases to production. They manage release schedules, coordinate across teams, mitigate deployment risks, and ensure smooth rollouts with clear communication.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release schedules and coordinate release windows
- Manage release branches, builds, and deployment pipelines
- Coordinate go/no-go decisions with stakeholders
- Document release notes and deployment procedures
- Monitor deployments and coordinate rollback if needed
- Track release metrics and post-release issues

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, predictable release cadence
- Minimize production incidents and rollbacks
- Maintain clear release documentation and communication
- Continuously improve deployment processes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and readiness reviews
- Release announcements and status updates
- Incident coordination during deployments
- Post-release retrospectives and metrics reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate code freeze, review changes, manage hotfixes
- **QA/Testing**: Verify release candidates, coordinate final testing cycles
- **Project Managers**: Align release timing with project milestones and dependencies
- **Product Managers**: Communicate feature delivery, coordinate customer communications
- **Stakeholders**: Provide release updates, manage expectations, communicate impacts

Stakeholders are individuals or groups with interest in or influence over project outcomes. They provide inputs, approvals, and feedback while being kept informed of progress and decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and constraints
- Review and approve key deliverables and decisions
- Offer domain expertise and feedback
- Communicate organizational priorities and changes
- Support project success through advocacy and resources

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver expected business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Provide timely feedback and approvals
- Remove organizational barriers to success

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Review sessions for key deliverables
- Feedback on prototypes and releases
- Escalation path for critical issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide approvals, review progress, communicate priorities
- **Product Managers**: Define success criteria, prioritize features, validate outcomes
- **Change Manager**: Review change impacts, provide feedback on transitions
- **Business Analyst**: Provide requirements, validate solutions, participate in UAT
- **Release Manager**: Approve release timing, communicate to end users

## Role Interaction Patterns

Understanding how roles collaborate is essential for clear accountability and smooth handoffs. Below are common interaction patterns across project phases:

### During Planning
- **Product Manager** defines features and priorities → **Business Analyst** documents detailed requirements → **UX Designer** creates design mockups → **Developers** estimate effort
- **Project Manager** coordinates timeline → **Scrum Master** facilitates sprint planning → **Release Manager** identifies release windows

### During Execution  
- **Developers** implement features → **UX Designer** reviews implementation → **QA/Testing** validates quality
- **Scrum Master** removes blockers → **Business Analyst** clarifies requirements → **Product Manager** makes trade-off decisions

### During Release
- **QA/Testing** signs off on quality → **Release Manager** coordinates deployment → **Project Manager** communicates status → **Stakeholders** provide final approval
- **Change Manager** communicates changes → **Product Manager** announces features → **Developers** provide deployment support

### Continuous Activities
- **Scrum Master** facilitates retrospectives → **Project Manager** tracks actions → **Change Manager** coordinates improvements
- **Business Analyst** validates business value → **Product Manager** measures outcomes → **Stakeholders** review results
